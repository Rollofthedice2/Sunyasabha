# Śūnyasabhā (The Assembly of Emptiness): shot list

Song: "शून्यसभा ŚŪNYASABHĀ (The Assembly of Emptiness)", 137 BPM. The song runs 0:00 to 3:07.3; the credits run 3:07.3 to 3:46.3. The film is detailed pixel art: a gothic cathedral turned into an illegal club, lit by red neon and blue flames, with two giant cracked stone heads flanking a DJ on a circuit-board throne. The crowd ranges from humans with small implants to the occasional full robot.

Each shot gives its start and end time (m:ss.s), its section of the song, what is on screen, and the lyric lines visible during it, in English as shown in the film, followed by the Sanskrit original. The same data is in `shotlist.json`.

**Recurring characters**

- **The DJ**: long black hair, large headphones, a dark robe over the left shoulder, a bare tattooed right arm, layered necklaces.
- **The titanium woman** (the "sister" of Verse 2): bald, a long braid, a segmented titanium spine down her bare back, round shoulder implants, black latex.
- **The young man with the eye-screen**: curly dark hair, a metal plate along one side of his face, a small transparent screen over one eye, a metal forearm.
- **The riot police**: black armour and helmets with a glowing red visor slit.
- **The man smoking outside**: leather jacket, dark red shirt; he sees the police coming and runs in to warn the club.
